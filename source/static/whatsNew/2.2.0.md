Moonfin 2.2.0 brings friends and chat from the Achievement Badges plugin to Roku, so you can see who's online, what they're watching, and message them. There's also a Home row of holiday movies, seasonal effects for the Home screen, a way to put your libraries in your own order, a way to hide parts of the details screen, and Live TV that reconnects by itself when a channel stalls. On the fix side, a video that never starts now falls back to a transcode, the track pickers work on every detail screen style, PGS subtitles show up, and Next Up works on Emby 4.10.

## Features

### Friends and Chat

- If your Jellyfin server runs the Achievement Badges plugin, open Settings > Integrations > Friends to see who's online and what they're watching, answer friend requests, and find people on your server to add.
- Chat with one friend or start a group. You can edit or delete your own messages, and photos your friends send show in the chat.
- A friend's profile shows their badges, points and best streak, and lets you open what they're watching.
- A banner in the top right corner tells you when a friend writes, wherever you are in the app. It stays quiet while a video plays.
- Privacy settings let you appear offline, hide what you're watching, and block people.
- Turn on Show friends button under Settings > Personalization > Navigation to add a Friends button that counts your requests and unread messages.

### Achievement Badges

- A banner shows in the top right corner when you unlock a badge. Turn it off with the Unlock notifications switch on the Achievement Badges screen, which changes it on your other devices too.

### Seasonal Row

- Turn on Seasonal Row under Settings > Integrations > External Lists > Seasonal Row for a Home row of holiday movies from your library, like Halloween, Thanksgiving and Christmas. With Seerr set up, it also suggests holiday movies you don't have yet.
- Pick your country so the row follows your holidays, and untick any holiday you don't want.
- It needs version 2.4.0 or newer of the Moonfin server plugin.

### Seasonal Effects

- Settings > Personalization > Seasonal Effects adds snow, fireworks, confetti, falling leaves, Halloween and more to the Home screen, with a Light, Normal or Heavy density. It's off by default.

### Live TV

- A channel that freezes or won't start is retried up to three times by itself, and the last try asks the server to convert the stream.
- If it still won't play, a Retry card comes up. Press OK to try again, or Up to pick another channel.

### Libraries and Home

- Library Order, under Settings > Personalization > Libraries, lets you put your libraries in your own order. It's saved to your server account, so your other apps use it too.
- Movies and shows you own show up in the IMDb and TMDB rows as your own copies, so they open and play from your library.
- Collection rows can follow the order you arranged the collection in on another Moonfin app. Pick Playlist Order under Collections Row Sorting.
- Sort & Filter has a Clear row in each group, and long lists like Genres and Tags have a search row.

### Details Screen

- Sections, under Settings > Personalization > Details Screen, lets you hide parts of the details screen, like the cast, chapters, extras or Seerr sections. It applies to every style.
- A movie that's in more than one collection shows all of them.
- Press Options on a playlist row to view its details, delete it from the playlist, or move it up or down.

### Setup Wizard

- The last step of the setup wizard lets you pick your audio language, subtitle mode and subtitle language. It's optional, and your choices are saved to your server account.

## Bug Fixes

### Playback

- A video the Roku can't open now falls back to a remux, then a full transcode, instead of ending in a playback error.
- Burn in subtitle when transcoding no longer makes the server convert the whole picture just for a text subtitle, which could stop the video from starting.
- PGS subtitles show. The server now burns them in, since the Roku can't draw them, and the Direct Play PGS Subtitles setting is gone.
- The audio track you pick is the one that plays, and switching tracks no longer reloads the video.
- With Custom Subtitles on, picking a subtitle no longer holds up the player while the server prepares it.

### Details Screen

- The subtitle, audio and version pickers work on the Nouveau, Minimalist and Spotlight styles.
- A downloaded subtitle shows up in the picker right away and gets selected.
- Favoriting a person no longer shows "This type is not yet supported: actor".
- The Favorite button on a playlist no longer crashes the app.
- Switching users no longer shows the last account's collections.
- Show Missing Collection Items no longer fills your own collections with a whole movie series.

### Live TV

- Leaving a channel frees the tuner on your server.
- Changing audio or subtitles on a live channel no longer makes the tuner reconnect.
- Some channels that showed "This type is not yet supported" now play.
- On Emby, stopping a live channel no longer cuts off someone else watching it.

### Home and Search

- Collection rows show only the collection's own titles, not every season of a series in it.
- Seerr, Upcoming Episodes and Upcoming Movies backdrops follow Browsing Backdrop Blur.
- Seerr, IMDb and TMDB cards on Modern rows show a backdrop instead of a stretched poster.
- TMDB TV chart rows open the right show.
- Blocked Ratings now cover the IMDb, TMDB and list rows.
- Search results come up faster on Emby and on Jellyfin before 12.
- Multi-Server Libraries keeps each server's library order.
- The Since you watched row title is translated.

### Emby

- The Next Up row fills on Emby 4.10.
- Libraries you hid from My Media stay hidden.
- Removing a title from My List works.
- Audio and subtitle language choices save to your Emby account.

### Settings Sync

- Subtitle Mode syncs properly with the other Moonfin apps.
