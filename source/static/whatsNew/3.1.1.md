Update the server plugin to [v3.1.1](https://github.com/Moonfin-Client/Plugin/releases/tag/3.1.1) too, or Moonbase for Silo and Plex 3.1.1 if you run Silo or Plex. Anything marked *(Moonbase)* below needs the plugin, and the JustWatch and TMDB rows need 3.1.1.

## Features

### Home
- A new Carousel row type puts a large details panel under each row's posters. It's the new default.
- New MDBList, JustWatch and TMDB rows show what's popular, including what's trending on Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+ and other streaming services. The row picker groups outside lists by source. *(Moonbase, and 3.1.1 for JustWatch and TMDB)*
- The MakD media bar is rebuilt to match MakD's v6 Classic layout, with Info, Play and Favorite buttons.
- The Banner media bar matches the size and placement it has in Moonfin Core.
- Seerr Discover rows follow Home's row spacing and Row Padding.
- Home loads faster at startup, on Silo too.

### Details and Library
- Collections open in Stage, Premiere, Frost, Quick Look and Minimalist with their cast and crew and a Remove from Collection menu, and big collections load 50 titles at a time.
- Modern and Classic details show the cast and crew of a title only Seerr has.
- Stage and Premiere draw Seerr's Recommendations and Similar rows with the Requests page's cards.
- Details pages load faster. On Silo, a long series' episodes show in about a quarter of the time.
- Poster rows on the details, person, library and Seerr pages keep focus on the left card and slide under it, like Home.
- Hide Watch Progress and Hide Unwatched Count work on the Library page too.

### Player
- Pausing shows the show's description, with a paused screen and preview that match the other Moonfin apps.
- The SyncPlay button can be hidden from the player buttons.

### Silo
- When a Silo admin ends your playback, the player closes and says why.
- Silo's own Home rows show their titles in the app's language.
- Silo search finds the same titles, people and requests as Silo's web app.
- Silo's 8-digit sign-in code is spaced 4 + 4 like Moonfin Core, so it fits beside the QR code.

### Privacy
- SponsorBlock lookups for trailers are hashed, so the trailer you're watching isn't sent.
- Logs scrub more secrets and email addresses.

### Other
- Settings for recommendations, the plugin and media segments no longer name Jellyfin, since they work on Emby too.

### Changed Defaults
- Carousel is the default row type.
- Trailer Preview in the media bar is off. Turn it back on in the media bar settings.

## Bug Fixes

### Playback
- A stream that fails partway through no longer restarts every few seconds and then from the start.
- A 4K remux that outgrows the Roku's buffer is converted in its own codec and capped at 1080p, instead of being sent as 4K H.264 that plays as a black screen.
- Live TV recovery stays on the stream the channel fell back to, and a stall right as a channel starts no longer stops it.
- The SyncPlay button only shows when SyncPlay is on and your account can use it.
- On Silo, the player shows the movie or series logo, and details screens no longer start a playback session on the server.

### Home
- Home rows match the Moonbase default rows the first time you sign in.
- Home rows that early November builds saved with a broken server name show up again.
- Game libraries no longer show in Recently Added and Recently Released, since Roku can't open games.
- Row Padding applies as soon as Settings closes instead of after a relaunch.
- On Silo, pinned collection, playlist and genre rows load.

### Details
- Changing the details style in Settings redraws the details page you had open.
- Stage, Nouveau and Minimalist open the season bar at the active season, and Nouveau's season bar stays on screen for a series with many seasons.
- Stage, Premiere and Quick Look draw their round buttons as circles and Play as a pill.
- Cast photos are round on 720p Rokus.
- On Silo, a series' logo shows on its episode details the first time they open, and personal ratings use Silo's 1 to 10 scale instead of saving half and showing double.

### Search
- Search shows both movies when two different movies share a title.
- Library search works on big libraries.
- Search result rows reach the right edge of the screen, episode cards without a picture show the series artwork, and the watched check sits in the card's corner.

### Seerr and Silo
- Accounts that sign in with SSO or Quick Connect get a Seerr session. *(Moonbase)*
- On Silo, Seerr titles no longer show the same titles in two rows, cast members open their person page, and requests show who made them instead of Unknown.
- Ending a Silo watch party frees its room in Moonbase, so hosts no longer run into the 10 rooms a day limit.

### Servers, Sign-in and Other
- Plex sign-in connects over HTTPS on a LAN instead of plain HTTP.
- Back on the server list closes the app instead of leaving a black screen.
- The Remove Server dialog keeps focus when server discovery finishes behind it.
- Keyboard Style works before you sign in.
- Chinese and other CJK text shows properly instead of as boxes.
- The library grid's focus ring is as thick as Home's.
