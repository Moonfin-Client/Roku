Now that all Moonfin clients are equal in terms of features, functionality and UI, all clients and the plugin are on the same release numbers and release schedule. So Roku goes straight from 2.2.0 to 3.0.0, along with Moonfin-Core, Smart-TV and the plugin.

Update the server plugin to 3.0.0 too. Sign in PINs on the server, Reviews, incomplete recordings, translated custom rows and most of the new synced settings need it.

Moonfin 3.0.0 brings Plex to Roku, next to Jellyfin and Emby. Settings, sign in and the setup wizard were rebuilt to match Moonfin-Core, the music player was rebuilt with a mini player that follows you around the app, and libraries get Classic, Modern and Docked styles. Live TV can rewind and record from the player, Home cards can play a preview or the whole trailer when focused, the player has an episode browser and dual subtitles, and sign in PINs can now live on your server. On the fix side, Seerr requests work with Windows root folders, deleted seasons can be requested again, hiding something from Continue Watching on Emby keeps your progress, a refused sign in says why, and Live TV from My Media no longer spins forever.

## Features

### Plex

- Moonfin signs in to Plex servers. Add Server has Sign in with Plex between Server Address and Emby Connect. Typing a Plex server's address, or picking a Plex server found on your network, also takes you to Plex sign in. Enter the code at plex.tv/link, scan the QR code with a phone that's signed in to Plex, or use your Plex email or username and password. Two-step verification works too.
- Pick your server and your user. Select a Server lists every server on your Plex account, owned and shared, and connects on its own when there's only one. Plex Home members show up on Who's watching? with their plex.tv avatars, and members with a PIN enter it to sign in.
- The best address, every time. Moonfin tries all of a server's addresses at once, local first and relays last, and checks them again on later launches. If your router blocks plex.direct names, it falls back to the plain local address.
- Browsing. Home has Continue Watching, Next Up and Latest rows, with card previews. Libraries have filters and sorting, and you can search and browse people, genres and studios. Favorites are saved as a 5 star rating in Plex, so anything you rated 5 stars there shows as a favorite.
- Details pages show HDR, Dolby Vision and frame rate, versions, extras, similar titles and theme songs. Plex has no trailer links of its own, so Moonfin finds each title's trailer with a YouTube search when there's no trailer extra.
- Playback. Files the Roku can play direct play, and everything else goes through Plex's transcoder. Subtitle files next to the video and embedded SRT show as text, and picture subtitles are burned in. Resume points and watched state sync with Plex, skip intro and credits use Plex's markers, seek previews show when Plex has built them, multi-part movies play their parts in order, and Cinema Mode plays Plex's cinema trailers and pre-roll.
- Hide from Continue Watching keeps your resume point on Plex.
- Plex doesn't get anything that needs the Moonbase plugin, like Settings Sync, Seerr, custom rows, extra ratings and Reviews. Live TV, Quick Connect, subtitle search, personal ratings, favorites on people, log uploads and the library filters Plex can't do aren't available on Plex either.

### Settings

- Settings were reorganized and redesigned to match Moonfin-Core. The top level is now Account, General, Appearance, Playback, Integrations and About, with the same sections, icons and wording as the other clients, so a setting lives in the same place everywhere.
- Choices open in a picker, and settings like Navbar Opacity and Row Padding are sliders you move with Left and Right.
- Rows, Action Buttons, Player Buttons and Metadata Row open as full screens with a Reset button.
- Plenty of labels match Core now, like Auto Login, Row Type and Media Bar Style.
- Pick your rating sources. Settings > Appearance > Details Page > Ratings > Rating Sources turns on and orders twelve sources, from IMDb and Rotten Tomatoes to Letterboxd and MyAnimeList. You couldn't change them on the Roku before. It syncs.
- Settings Sync can pick a profile. Settings > Account > Settings Sync shows the Moonbase connection and a Sync switch, with tiles for the Global, Desktop, Mobile and TV profiles. This Roku syncs with the profile you pick, with Load, Save and Reset. Before, it always used the TV profile. The pick stays on this Roku.
- Links you can scan. Settings > About has Source Code, Report an Issue, Join Discord, Licenses and Privacy Policy, and each one shows a QR code to open on your phone. Support Moonfin uses the same dialog.

### Sign In and Accounts

- The server list, Add Server, Who's watching?, sign in and the user switcher were redesigned to match the other clients. Add Server asks whether you're connecting with a server address, Plex or Emby Connect, and shows the server's name, type and version before anyone signs in. To remove a saved server, hold OK on it.
- Sign in PINs can live on your server. With Moonbase, a PIN set under Settings > Account > PIN Code works on every device, each account has its own, and the server counts wrong guesses. Without the plugin, each account's PIN is kept on this Roku. Turning the PIN off, changing it or removing it asks for the current one first. If you used PIN Code Protection before, your PIN keeps working and moves to the server the first time you enter it.
- Forgot PIN? on the PIN screen signs you in again with your password or Quick Connect and clears the PIN. A PIN kept on the server can only be cleared by a server administrator, and the screen says so.
- A QR code for Quick Connect. It opens your server's approval page with the code filled in, so a phone that's already signed in approves with one tap.
- A Time's up screen when a Jellyfin access schedule ends. Playback stops, the screen says when the account can be used next, and Switch User is right there. It clears on its own when the hours open again. The schedule is checked every minute and whenever the server refuses a request.
- The setup wizard was redesigned. Each step lists its options next to a large live preview, with a short hint for each option, a step count and where to find it in Settings later. You can run it again from Settings > About > Run setup again.

### Keyboard

- Keyboard styles. Settings > General > Input > Keyboard Style offers Native, the Roku's own keyboard, Classic, the original Moonfin keyboard, Glass, a frosted panel with larger keys, and Docked, the new default, which fills the bottom of the screen and shows what you type above the keys. A key on the keyboard itself switches style, and it syncs.

### Home

- Media previews on Home cards. Settings > Appearance > Home Screen > Media Preview offers Off, Media Preview or Trailer Preview on Modern rows. Media Preview plays a short clip of the focused card after about a second, from your resume point or a few minutes in, and a series plays its Next Up episode. Trailer Preview plays the whole trailer on the card, using the series trailer for episodes, and Seerr, IMDb, TMDB and seasonal cards play their TMDB trailer. It's off by default, with Preview Audio beside it.
- Peek Home Rows. Settings > Appearance > Home Screen > Media Bar > Peek Home Rows shows the top half of your first row along the bottom of the media bar. It's off by default and isn't offered for Banner. While a Moonfin or MakD media bar trailer plays, the row and the navigation bar step aside and the info box drops to the bottom of the screen.
- Home Screen Backdrop has four styles. Off, Full Screen, the default, Corner, which sits in the top right and fades toward the left and bottom, and Top, which keeps the top half of the picture and fades it toward the middle. Corner and Top apply while you're in the rows.
- Backdrops turn off one screen at a time. The backdrop switches are now Home Screen Backdrop, Details Screen Backdrop under Details Page for the Classic style, and Library Backdrops under Libraries, all synced through Moonbase. If you'd turned on Hide Backdrops while Browsing before, turn off Library Backdrops.
- The Rows editor was reworked. Settings > Appearance > Home Screen > Rows lists the rows you have on at the top in Home's order, and the rest under headers you open and close: General, Collections, Favorites, Genres and Studios, Playlists, Seerr and External Lists. The Radarr and Sonarr calendars sit with Seerr, and Live TV and On Now hide when the server has no channels.
- Every collection, genre and playlist is its own row in the editor, replacing the Pick Collections, Pick Genres and Pick Playlists menus. Collections from another signed in server show while Multi-Server Libraries is on. Renamed picks take their new name, and picks the server no longer has are removed. On Now is its own entry too, so it can sit apart from Live TV.
- Separate ratings for Home and the media bar. Home Screen and Media Bar each have a Ratings section with Additional Ratings, Rating Sources, Rating Labels and Rating Badges, and Home also gets Episode Ratings. Before, both followed the Details Page settings. The new sections start from the defaults, so if you'd trimmed your sources before, set them again here.
- A redesigned Classic info area. Classic rows show the title's logo, or a bigger title when there's none. An episode leads with S1:E2 and its name, then the year, rating, runtime, resolution and genres on one line. Ratings sit on a single row, the description runs up to three lines, and Classic cards get a second line under the name with the year or episode.
- External Lists in the media bar. Settings > Appearance > Home Screen > Media Bar > External Lists mixes the IMDb and TMDB charts and your custom lists into the media bar, or lets them fill it on their own. When a title is in your library, its library copy wins. Selecting an outside title opens it in Seerr. (Moonbase)
- Sort IMDb, TMDB and Seerr rows by Default (List Order), Name, Release Date, Rating, Popularity or Shuffle, ascending or descending. Find it in IMDb Lists, TMDB Lists and Seerr Rows under Home Screen > External Sources. The whole list is sorted before the row is cut, ties go to the name, and it syncs.
- Custom rows in your language. IMDb and TMDB chart rows, custom rows and the media bar's External Lists ask Moonbase for titles in your Roku's language. (Moonbase)
- Seerr's custom Discover sliders, the ones a Seerr admin adds, show on Discover after its own rows. You can add them as Home rows from Home Screen > Seerr Rows > Seerr Discover Sliders, and they follow Seerr when a slider is renamed, turned off or deleted.
- Play straight from Continue Watching and Next Up. Settings > Appearance > Home Screen > Row Options > Default Action picks Go to Details, the default, or Play immediately. With Play immediately, the card menu offers Go to Details instead. It syncs.
- Pressing Home on the navigation bar while you're on Home brings up a fresh set of media bar titles, and keeps the current set if the new draw comes back empty.
- Banner art in the Banner media bar. Titles with banner art show the whole banner over a blurred copy of it, and the rest keep the backdrop with the logo or title.
- Tighter Home spacing. Modern rows default to the smallest Row Padding, and Classic rows sit closer together.

### Seasonal Effects

- Five new seasonal effects. Valentine's Day hearts, painted Easter eggs, Pride confetti, Lunar New Year lanterns and Diwali lights join the list under Settings > Appearance > Seasonal Effects.
- Effects that follow your focus on Home. While its effect is on, a little companion follows the focused card or navigation button. It's a Halloween planchette, Cupid's arrow, a fireworks sparkler, Santa's sleigh, Easter bunny ears, a lantern, a diya or a rainbow. Focus Follower under Seasonal Effects turns it off and keeps the effect.

### Libraries

- Classic, Modern and Docked library styles. Settings > Appearance > Libraries > Video Library Style changes the header on your movie and show grids. Classic is the original, Modern puts labeled Sort, Show and Display buttons in one bar, and Docked moves everything into a side panel with a letter grid. It syncs.
- The library screen matches the other clients. Sort & Filter, Group By and Display are separate dialogs.
- Group By splits a movie or TV library by decade, genre, parental rating or studio, as rows or as chips with counts.
- Display holds the scroll direction, now with Horizontal, Poster, Thumbnail or the new Banner cards, the poster size, the alphabet bar and Folder View.
- New sorts include My Rating, Folders First, and Track Number and Length for songs.
- Each library keeps its own filters and sort, so Forget Filters is gone. Home videos and mixed libraries open in Folder View.
- Titles Under Posters and the poster size sync, as one setting for every library instead of one per library.
- Every library on the navigation bar. Show Libraries Individually, under Settings > Appearance > Navigation Bar, gives each library its own button on the top bar or sidebar with an icon for its type. It shows while Show Libraries in Toolbar is on, is off by default and syncs.
- The Favorites screen matches the other clients. Sort and Settings open in a side panel, the grid can scroll sideways, and the A to Z strip narrows what's shown. With more than one server signed in, each card shows its server and a title on two servers shows once.

### Music

- The music player was rebuilt. A big cover sits over a blurred copy with the title, artist, album and a quality badge like FLAC 24/96 Lossless. Its round buttons cover Favorite, Add to Playlist, Shuffle, Previous, Play and Pause, Next, Repeat with a repeat one mode, Lyrics and Up Next. Lyrics fill the right side and follow the song, and Up Next shows the queue.
- Music keeps going while you browse. A music pill under the top navigation bar, or a music card at the bottom of the sidebar, controls what's playing from any screen and opens the player. It replaces the old bottom mini player.
- Three music library styles, under Settings > Appearance > Libraries > Music Library Style. Classic is the original screen. Quick Access adds a Jump Back In grid of what you played recently. Immersive, the new default, leads with the album you're listening to, then From This Album, Latest, Last Played and Browse By Genre.
- Music categories open full screens in Quick Access and Immersive. Artists have a letter rail, Albums filter by genre, Songs starts with four mixes, and Playlists, Genres and Favorites get pages of their own.

### Details Screen

- A Reviews section with TMDB reviews as cards you open to read in full. It's on Classic, Modern and Nouveau, and Spotlight has a Reviews card. It can be hidden under Settings > Appearance > Details Page > Sections. (Moonbase)
- A trailer picker. Titles with more than one trailer open a list with each trailer's length, channel and a Subtitled tag. Details trailers follow Trailer Captions now too.
- Collections play as a playlist. Modern collections get a Playlist tab and Spotlight a Playlist Order card, each with a sort button: Server Order, the new default, Release Order, Alphabetical, and Custom Order when one is saved through Moonbase.
- Picking a title in a collection plays from there with the rest queued, and Play follows the saved custom order.
- Missing titles from Seerr now slot in by release on every style.
- Nouveau movie and show pages get a rail for each collection they're in, and show pages list their collections in every style.
- People open in your detail style, with a new Display button next to Favorite. Display sorts their credits alphabetically or by release date and can group repeated roles, and both choices sync. Modern opens a Modern page, Spotlight and Minimalist open Spotlight with a Filmography card, and Classic and Nouveau show each title's role under it.
- Sort and filter playlists. Classic and Modern playlist pages get Sort, Filter and Reset. Sort by name, artist, album, runtime, date added, release date, ratings, last played, play count or at random, and filter by played status, favorites or type. Play All and Shuffle follow what's shown, and the playlist on the server never changes. Under Spotlight, Minimalist and Nouveau, playlists open in the Modern layout.
- Spotlight has a File Details card for movies and episodes that opens the full file information, and a version badge next to the title when there's more than one version, with a Version badge switch under Sections. Its cards keep to 16:9 and slide sideways, each with its own backdrop.
- The episode you're viewing is marked in its season's list, with the border on Modern and a blue band on Minimalist and Spotlight.
- Minimalist movie pages get an Extras button that opens the extras in a panel. It's hidden in Kids Mode.
- Season pages get Go to Series in every style, and the Classic season page follows your Action Buttons order and count, with a More menu for the rest.
- Seerr titles show where they stream. A Streaming on row lists the subscription services Seerr has for your region, with a Streaming services switch under Sections.
- Anime markers can use the short Sub, Dub and Sub/Dub labels when the plugin asks for them.

### Seerr

- One request sheet. Quality, seasons and the advanced options sit on a single sheet, with a 4K switch, All Seasons, season chips that mark what you already have, and how many requests you have left. Report Issue, Manage Requests and Cancel Request got matching dialogs, and a failed request tells you why.
- Request a whole collection. A collection's Seerr page has Request Collection, which lets you tick the films you want and sends them one at a time.
- Season and episode pages get the Seerr actions, and Report Issue starts on that season and episode.
- Request actions sit on the poster as round buttons on the Requests screen, and anyone can cancel their own pending request from there.
- Seerr Discover follows your Home row style, drawing Modern rows when Home uses them. Seerr, chart and calendar cards on Home use the TMDB backdrop with the title in it.
- A Poster Size for Seerr browsing. The gear on a Seerr genre, studio, network or collection grid sets Small, Medium, Large or Extra Large.

### Playback

- An episode browser in the player. The new Episodes button opens the season that's playing, scrolled to the current episode, with season tabs, progress bars and anime markers. Picking an episode plays it from where you left off. Place the button under Settings > Playback > Player > Player Buttons.
- The skip prompt works like Moonfin-Core's. It shows with the player controls up, just above the seek bar, and Up from the seek bar reaches it. It's offered on the way into a segment, Back hides it before it closes the controls, and it stays out of the way right after you seek.
- Auto Hide Skip Button, under Settings > Playback > Skipping & Autoplay, hides the skip prompt after 5 or 10 seconds. After that it only comes back with the controls until the segment ends. It's off by default and syncs.
- Holding Left or Right speeds up seeking. The first few moves use your step, then it goes 30, 60 and 180 seconds at a time, and it stops when you let go.
- A Cinema Mode Skip Button. Under Settings > Playback > Skipping & Autoplay, it shows a Skip button for the first ten seconds of each pre-roll. Right brings it back, Back hides it, and Fast Forward skips the pre-roll. The player controls open on pre-rolls now too.
- A light dim behind the player controls keeps them readable over bright scenes.
- A rebuilt Playback Information dialog with Playback, Video, Audio, Subtitles, File and Technical sections. It explains the play method in a plain sentence and says in plain words why a file is transcoding, like a codec the Roku can't play or a bitrate over your limit. It also shows what the server converted from, and the channel and program for Live TV.
- Convert just the audio. Settings > Playback > Audio > Transcode Audio Codecs has the server convert the audio codecs you pick while the video plays untouched. Music still direct plays.
- Your audio and subtitle picks are remembered for that title, and for the whole series on an episode, ahead of your language settings. Turning subtitles off is remembered too. This stays on the Roku.

### Subtitles

- Dual subtitles. Turn on Secondary Subtitles under Settings > Playback > Subtitles, and the Subtitles button asks for the primary or secondary track. A second text track stacks right above the first with its own offset, size and color. Preferred Secondary Subtitle Language puts a language at the top of the list, and your pick is remembered for each title.
- Search in several languages at once. Subtitle Download Languages, under Settings > Playback > Subtitles, searches up to five languages when you download a subtitle. Auto keeps searching your Default Subtitle Language.
- A Fetching subtitles indicator shows while a subtitle you picked is still loading. It waits half a second so quick ones don't make it flash, and it moves to the corner once the controls hide.
- The subtitle list leads with your languages. None comes first, then your Default Subtitle Language, your fallback language and full tracks ahead of commentary.
- The Offset row shows the current offset, and subtitle download says Search using OpenSubtitles.

### Live TV

- Rewind live TV. Once a stream keeps at least a minute, the player gets a seek bar from the start of that window to live, showing the time of day and how far behind live you are. Seek back, Seek forward and Jump to Live buttons come with it, and Left and Right skip inside the window. A channel played as a plain transport stream keeps no window, so it can't rewind.
- Record from the player. A Record button in the Live TV controls records the program airing now, or its series, and can cancel either one. It turns red while recording.
- Choose which episodes a series records. Record Series asks for All Episodes, Episodes Not Already Recorded or New Episodes Only. Series Settings changes that later from the guide, the player or Recordings > Series Recordings.
- Series recordings are marked in the guide, its preview band and the channel changer, grayed out on a showing the rule will skip.
- Record now, later or both. Press Record on something that's already started, and Moonfin shows the airing now next to a future showing, with how closely the two match. Record either one or both. (Moonbase)
- Incomplete recordings. A recording that missed part of its program gets an Incomplete badge, and its details say how much was recorded and whether a complete showing is scheduled. Live TV managers can choose Keep as Complete on the details page or the Recordings screen. (Moonbase)
- Episode lines in the guide. Guide cells show the episode title and its S1:E5 number where the rating and category used to be, and the line scrolls when the cell is focused.
- Live TV Direct Play. Under Settings > Playback > Quality & Decoding, it plays tuner channels as the transport stream they come in. With MPEG-2 on under Video Codec Support, MPEG-2 antenna channels from a tuner like an HDHomeRun direct play instead of being transcoded to H.264. It's off by default and syncs.

## Bug Fixes

### Playback

- A subtitle you picked no longer carries over to the next title. Only the audio pick was cleared when you moved on.
- Subtitles stop picking a forced track over the full one when a file only says forced in the track's title. Only Forced mode now picks a forced track in a language you read, your subtitle language, fallback language or the audio playing, and leaves subtitles off when there's none.
- Subtitle downloads search your Default Subtitle Language first, so a language the file doesn't carry yet still gets searched. Dutch language codes are read properly now.
- Recordings still in progress play. When playback info for the pinned source fails, Moonfin asks again without it.
- Mute Trailer Audio only mutes the media bar, and trailers opened from Details play with sound.

### Live TV

- Live TV from My Media no longer spins forever. The spinner stayed up and held the remote.
- The remote works after closing the channel changer in the Live TV player.
- Search finds every program. Live TV program search only found programs that weren't a movie, series, sports, news or kids program. Program results show the episode line now too.
- The guide stops repeating a program's name as its episode title.
- The Recordings schedule only lists what's still going to record. Canceled, finished and failed timers no longer show or count, and the schedule reloads after a series rule changes.

### Emby

- Channel items like a HyperMS library play. Moonfin asked for their placeholder source, which the server could only fail to transcode.
- Hiding from Continue Watching or Next Up keeps your progress. It uses Emby's own hide, instead of marking the item unwatched.
- Creating a playlist works on Emby, and new playlists on Jellyfin are private instead of public.

### Home

- Ratings under a focused Modern card stay on one line, and the description runs across the row.
- Home sections that come back empty stop flashing their titles on every load.
- Home loads faster with lots of picked rows. Collection, genre and playlist rows below the first eight sections load three at a time as focus comes near them.
- The media bar loads faster. It reads six libraries at a time and stops once it has enough titles.

### Details

- Picking the episode you're on from its own list plays it instead of reopening its page.
- Back leaves a run of episodes in one press. Opening an episode from another episode's page replaces it now, where each one used to stack up.
- Special features are listed in order, by their number and then by name.
- Spotlight puts episodes with no season under Specials, and orders episodes that share a number by title.
- Missing dates stop showing as year 1 on person pages and in a file's Added date.
- Cast and crew names no longer overlap their photos on Modern and Spotlight.

### Libraries

- The Genres view in a library works. Its focus ring sat below the tile, and picking a genre in a library opened from the navigation bar did nothing.
- Genre and studio browses keep their own sort, filters and view instead of overwriting the library's.
- Library Image Orientation applies to library grids. The main grid ignored it.
- The ratings row hides when Show Media Details is off.
- Multi-Server Libraries opens a genre across every server, asking each one by name, instead of only the active server. A library from another server, and the folders inside it, load from that server.

### Seerr

- Requests work with Windows root folders. Paths like C:\Media broke the request and Seerr answered with an error.
- Deleted and blocklisted seasons can be requested again. They counted as already in the library, and seasons from a failed request stayed locked.
- Cancel Request only shows for requests you can cancel. It showed whenever anyone had a pending request.
- Download progress is right. A season pack counts once instead of once per episode, and releases Sonarr or Radarr is holding back are left out.
- View Collection opens the collection. The banner did nothing on Classic, Modern and Nouveau.
- Seerr's Recently Added row has its titles and artwork.
- The Browse Movies by Genre and Browse TV by Genre rows load. They were always empty.
- Radarr releases stay on the calendar row through their own day. West of UTC, a release dropped off the evening before.

### Sign In

- A refused sign in says why. Jellyfin refuses an account that's outside its allowed hours, locked or disabled, and the sign in screen called that a wrong password. Quick Connect also stops waiting when the server refuses after you approve the code.
