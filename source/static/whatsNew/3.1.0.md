## Features

### Servers
- Moonfin on Roku works with Silo servers, including Silo's own watch parties.
- Smart Search lets you search by describing what you want in a sentence, once your admin turns it on in Moonbase. It works on Plex too through Moonbase for Plex. *(Moonbase)*

### Watching Together
- Watch Parties come to Jellyfin, Emby and Plex, with a room list, a Home row, lobby voting, chat, reactions and synced playback. Turn them on under Settings > Playback > Watch Parties. *(Moonbase)*
- Jellyfin SyncPlay comes to Roku, with a groups panel, group-controlled playback and drift correction. (#150)
- Details screens have Watch with group.

### Home
- A new Watching on MDBList row shows what MDBList users are watching right now. *(Moonbase)*
- The media bar labels series with New Series, New Season or New Episodes. It now draws from the libraries and collections you pick, with Random, Recently Added, Recently Released and New Seasons & Episodes sources.
- The Radarr and Sonarr upcoming rows hide titles already on your server, and dates read Today, Tomorrow or In 3 days.
- Admins can add poster labels like 4K, HDR or Atmos in Moonbase. *(Moonbase)*
- The Studios row shows TMDB studio logos from Moonbase, with dark logos in white.
- Card Size and Image Type per Row work like they do in Moonfin Core.
- Row Options has Hide Watched for the Recently Added and Recently Released rows, and it follows your server's own setting.
- The audio rows can be sorted, and the Collections rows have a Sort Order.
- IMDb, TMDB and other external rows no longer need their own switches. They show up in the Home Rows editor whenever the server can fill them.
- Home Row Info Overlay lets you turn off the info area over Classic rows.
- The seasonal row's Show All Year setting shows a holiday row outside the holiday dates too.
- The row counters next to row titles are gone.

### Details
- There are four new details styles, Stage, Premiere, Quick Look and Frost. Frost is the new default.
- File badges show the Dolby Vision profile, like DV P8.

### Search and Seerr
- Search has an Audiobooks row.
- Seerr Discover and Browse were rebuilt to match Moonfin Core's TV layout.
- Seerr's status on posters can show as a corner dot or a banner, with the new Badge Style setting.
- The Seerr download bar says why a download is stuck, and admins can grab a different release. *(Moonbase)*

### Calendar
- A new Calendar screen shows upcoming and recently aired episodes and movies from Sonarr and Radarr. *(Moonbase)*
- Date Format picks month and day, day and month, or your locale's default.

### Player
- The player controls were rebuilt to match Moonfin Core's TV layout.
- The chapter picker is now a row of chapter cards.

### Settings and Setup
- Settings shows a live preview of what each setting changes, and the pickers and checklists have a new look.
- Who's watching and Switch User are now a carousel, like Moonfin Core and Smart-TV.
- The last setup step can add Moonbase to your server.
- Press Settings > About > Version seven times to open After Hours, a hidden arcade with mini games.

### Changed Defaults
- Frost is the default details style.
- My Media uses landscape thumbnails. Turn on Modern cards on My Media row to get posters back.
- Rating badges on the details page are off. Turn Rating Badges back on in the Details Page settings.

## Bug Fixes

### Playback
- Dolby Vision Profile 7 and out-of-spec Profile 8 files direct play from their HDR10 base layer on HDR10 TVs, instead of a black screen or an SDR transcode. (#326)
- Video that's too big for the Roku's buffer now converts on the server instead of failing over and over.
- The seek preview shows thumbnails on 720p Roku devices.
- The track pickers put focus on the list when they open.

### Home
- Home starts faster, with fewer duplicate requests at startup.
- Changing Home rows in Settings shows on Home right away.
- With several servers, the Favorites and Recently Released rows are sorted across all of them.
- Recently Added rows load more as you reach their end.
- Home posters that fail to load try again.
- Thumb rows draw wide cards instead of posters squeezed into the frame.

### Details and Libraries
- Big collections open on Modern instead of timing out. (#327)
- Picking another version updates the badges under the title.
- An episode's season list opens on that episode.
- Watched checks on episode cards and the details screens' buttons, focus rings and chips follow your theme.
- Episode pages show the series' cast along with the guest stars.
- On Emby, the focused item in a library shows its year, ratings and series status.
- Search on Jellyfin shows movies and series again when lots of episodes or chapters match.

### Seerr
- Seerr requests, watchlist adds and issue reports work again on Roku OS 15.
- Watchlisted shows in Favorites show their names.

### Navigation and Sign-in
- Quick Connect opens on its own tab instead of on Back.
- Who's watching lists users in the same order as the account switcher.
- Avatars are the right shape on 720p screens.
- With a theme on, the focused navbar button uses the theme's surface color instead of looking like a progress bar.
- The Home layout syncs through Moonbase under the right setting names.
