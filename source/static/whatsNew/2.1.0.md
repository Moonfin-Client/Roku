Moonfin 2.1.0 brings Roku up to date with Moonfin 2.6.0 on your other devices, and it adds something Roku never had: Emby support. There are also three new detail screen styles, Kids Mode with a PIN, Achievement Badges, anime episode markers, a rebuilt Live TV guide, and trailers from YouTube. On the fix side, titles from a second server now play from the right server, resume picks up where you left off again, and the Error During Playback some people saw when starting a video is fixed.

## Features

### Emby Servers

- Moonfin on Roku now works with Emby. Pick a server found on your network, choose Enter Server URL, or choose Emby Connect to sign in with your Emby account and pick one of your servers. Your Emby Connect password isn't saved.
- Browsing, home rows, search, favorites, detail screens and playback all work, including resume, versions, chapters, seek previews, and Skip Intro and Credits.
- With the Moonfin server plugin on your Emby server, you also get settings sync, ratings, themes, admin messages and Seerr.
- Quick Connect and lyrics stay Jellyfin only, since Emby doesn't have them.

### Three New Detail Screen Styles

- Spotlight puts the artwork first, with a row of cards under it that each open a panel for seasons, cast and crew, chapters and extras, collections, recommendations and Seerr details.
- Nouveau opens on a full screen image, then lays out every section as its own row down one page.
- Minimalist keeps it simple: the artwork, the title, one row of buttons and the episodes.
- Pick one under Settings > Personalization > Details Screen > Detail Screen Style, or in the setup wizard. Roku now has the same five styles as the other Moonfin apps.

### Detail Screens

- The Details tab on Modern shows file information, like the name, size, format, audio and subtitle tracks, and whether it plays directly. When it doesn't, it tells you why and which setting would change that.
- Choose how many buttons stay on screen before the rest move into More Actions.
- Choose which details show under the title, like the year, rating, runtime, genres and Seerr status, and put them in your own order.
- Series show when the next episode airs, like Next: Thursday (S2:E4).
- Collections open on their own detail screen, and Play picks up the movie you're partway through or the next unwatched one. A movie also shows the collection it belongs to.
- Show Missing Collection Items, under Settings > Integrations > Seerr, fills in the movies from a collection you don't have yet.
- The card menu has more options, like Hide from Continue Watching, Add to Collection, and for admins Refresh Metadata, Identify and Change Artwork. It now works in search, favorites and more places.
- Watched checks, unplayed counts and favorite hearts are easier to read, and the favorite heart now shows on home rows too.
- Recommendations have new sources that match the other Moonfin apps, and More Like This shows up to twenty titles.

### Kids Mode and Parental Controls

- Kids Mode, under Settings > Account & Security > Kids Mode, strips the app back for a child. It hides Seerr, Live TV, the library list and most of Settings, keeps a simple Home screen, and uses the Minimalist detail screen. Getting out needs a four digit PIN.
- Kids Mode stays on for this Roku, even if you switch accounts or sign out. Too many wrong PINs locks you out for a while.
- PIN Code Protection is a new option that asks for a PIN every time someone signs in or switches accounts. It's off by default.
- Blocked Ratings lets you block content ratings. Blocking one also blocks every stronger rating, and blocked titles disappear from everywhere in the app.

### Achievement Badges

- If your Jellyfin server runs the Achievement Badges plugin, open Settings > Integrations > Achievement Badges to see your rank, streaks, badges, quests, the leaderboard and more.

### Anime Markers

- Anime episodes can show Filler, Canon and Recap markers, plus whether they're Subbed or Dubbed. Your server admin turns them on in the Moonfin server plugin.

### Live TV

- The guide was rebuilt. It's full screen, previews the program you're on, and filters by Movies, Series, Sports, News, Kids and more. Page through the times, jump up to two weeks ahead, or type a channel number to go straight to it. It opens on the channel you last watched.
- Select a program to watch it, record it, or record the whole series.
- A new Recordings screen shows recent recordings, TV series, what's coming up and your series recordings.
- Press Up while watching live TV to flip through channels without leaving the player.
- The live player shows the channel number, name and program.
- A Live TV button in the navigation bar goes straight to the guide.

### Trailers

- The Trailer button now plays trailers from YouTube when your server doesn't have one, at up to 1080p and in the original language.
- Trailers play behind the media bar after a few seconds in the Moonfin, MakD, Aya and Gallery styles. Trailer Preview, Mute Trailer Audio and Trailer Captions are in the media bar settings.

### Player

- Scrubbing shows a preview of where you'll land, and a new Seek Step setting picks how far each press moves: 5, 10, 20 or 30 seconds.
- A chapter menu lists every chapter with its start time. Chapter Marks, off by default, shows chapters on the seek bar.
- A new loading animation replaces Roku's loading bars, and you can change how it looks.
- The player shows a bigger title logo.
- Custom Subtitles can be restyled, with their own size, position, colors and background.

### Home and Libraries

- Favorites was redesigned, with a backdrop and a tab for each kind of item.
- Playlists group into rows by type.
- Modern home rows fade smoothly from one card to the next.
- Recently Added TV rows show one card per series.
- New library options let you group items into collections, or use plain landscape tiles on the My Media row.
- You can read messages from your server admin. Turn on Show messages button under Settings > Personalization > Navigation.
- Show Clock, under General Style, can hide the clock.
- This What's New popup now scrolls.
- On Jellyfin 12, search includes results from server plugins.

## Bug Fixes

### Multiple Servers

- Movies, shows, music and audiobooks from a second server now play from that server, along with their subtitles, intros and next episodes.
- Ratings, Delete, Add to Playlist and the other detail screen actions now go to the right server.
- The multi-server Next Up row loads again, and extras show on detail screens.
- Trailers in another server's library are found.
- Your server sign-in is no longer sent to other websites when a file links to one.

### Playback

- Fixed Error During Playback when starting a video. Moonfin now asks the server for a fresh stream from where you were, with the same audio and subtitles.
- Resume picks up where you left off again from a series, season or episode screen.
- You can turn autoplay off. The new Autoplay Next Episode setting is under Automation & Queue. Before this, Show Next Episode Details After Finish was controlling autoplay by mistake.
- Choosing None turns subtitles off, and your Roku's own caption setting comes back after playback.
- A downloaded subtitle gets selected, and subtitle search starts in the language you're watching.
- Some embedded subtitles that wouldn't show now play.
- The subtitle offset can be opened again, from the Offset row in the subtitle picker, and it carries over to the next episode.

### Details and Seerr

- Seasons you don't have no longer show as watched.
- MyAnimeList and RT Audience ratings no longer show twice.
- A person's age is right before their birthday.
- Recommendations are sorted by how well they match, instead of in random order.
- The Guest Appearances and Music Videos tabs on a person's page fill in.
- Cast and crew photos on Modern aren't cut off at the top.
- Detail Screen Style syncs properly with the Samsung and LG app.
- Genre counts are right in big libraries, and genre tiles have backdrops.
- Studio logos load on Jellyfin 12.
- Seerr sign-in no longer freezes after you enter your username.
- Pickers in the Seerr request dialog, like Quality Profile, respond to the remote.

### Home, Libraries and Sign-in

- Back returns to the media bar after you open something from it.
- The media bar works with mixed content libraries, instead of showing a black bar.
- The Gallery media bar's genre glow works.
- Marking something watched or as a favorite shows everywhere right away.
- Library search finds matches beyond what's loaded so far, and it ignores accents.
- The library column count sliders are gone, since they did nothing.
- Picking a profile on Who's watching no longer freezes the sign-in screen.
- Switching users no longer turns Auto Sign In back on.
- Entering a server address no longer hangs forever. It gives up after 45 seconds.
- Home loads faster when the Rewatch row has large collections.

### Reliability

- Fixed a batch of crashes reported on 2.0.0.
- Diagnostic logs hide server addresses, IP addresses and sign-in tokens before they're shown or sent.
- More of the subtitle download screens can be translated.

Heads up: More Like This and Since You Watched now use Moonfin Recommends by default (it used to be called Local) instead of Jellyfin Recommends (it used to be called Server). You can switch back in the recommendation settings.
